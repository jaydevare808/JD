PaperNote AI Lite

PaperNote includes an optional on-device AI assistant. On supported devices, the bundled language model runs locally inside the app. Questions and generated responses stay on the device and are not sent to a PaperNote server. The AI feature may use device CPU resources, memory, battery and storage. AI output can be incorrect and should be checked against trusted academic material.

PaperNote Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 September 2026

PaperNote is an offline-first handwriting notebook application.

Data collection

PaperNote does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information through a server operated by the app.

Local storage

Your notebooks, handwriting pages, and app settings are stored locally on your Android device. PaperNote provides export and backup features so you can create your own copies.

Permissions

The app may request storage access only on older Android versions when required to export files. No contacts, location, microphone, camera, SMS, call logs, or advertising identifiers are required by PaperNote.

Third-party services

The stable Play Store build does not use Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, or cloud notebook synchronization.

Updates

App updates are delivered through Google Play. The stable Play build does not download or install application updates from third-party servers.

Contact

For PaperNote support or privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact information shown on the Google Play listing.
